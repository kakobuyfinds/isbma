Sizing, QC photos and shipping

How do I check the size?

For shoes, compare your foot length with the seller’s size chart and ask for an insole measurement if you are between sizes. For clothing, lay a similar garment flat and compare its chest width, body length and sleeve length in centimeters.

What should I look for in QC photos?

Match the photos to the color and size you selected. For shoes, check both heels and outsoles. For hoodies and tees, check seam alignment and print placement. For jackets, ask for close-ups of zippers, cuffs and lining if they are unclear.

Why can the shipping quote change?

Final packaging can change the parcel’s weight and dimensions. Ask whether your route bills by actual or volumetric weight, then refresh the estimate once you have the packed dimensions.
